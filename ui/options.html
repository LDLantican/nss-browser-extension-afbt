Troubleshooting

Nothing here is needed day to day. Approved work is billed by itself every minute, and the web app's delivery queue lists anything that needs a person. These are for finding out why something did not go through.

Check an AppFolio vendor page

Reads the vendor-portal tab you used most recently and lists which of the appfolio_vendor adapter's hooks it finds there. It clicks and types nothing, so it is safe on a real client's work order.
